Questions You're Probably Asking

Is this just another course or coaching program?

No. We don't hand you a framework and wish you luck. We build every system inside your operation — using your tools, your clients, your team — and stay through Day 75 to make sure your team actually adopts it.

I've tried SOPs and project management tools before. Nobody used them.

Systems fail without enforcement, not because your team is lazy. We build the accountability framework alongside every SOP, so using it isn't optional — and adoption is tracked from day one, not assumed.

My operation is messy. I'm embarrassed to show anyone.

The messier it is, the more we can fix. Every agency we've worked with looked the same way going in. We've never seen an operation too broken to install this in.

Will this disrupt my team or slow down client delivery?

No. The build happens in the first 14 days with minimal time required from your team. Training in Phase 3 fits inside the existing workday — total team time is a few hours across the week, not days off delivery.

How is this different from just hiring an ops person?

An ops hire still depends on you to know what "good" looks like — and they leave. We install the system and the enforcement mechanism directly into your business, train your existing team on it, and hand over full documentation. It doesn't walk out the door.

What if my team pushes back on new systems?

Expected, and planned for. We script the rollout, train the team directly, and build accountability around their own performance goals — it reads as ownership, not surveillance. People who quit over clear expectations were never going to stay.

What happens after the 75 days? Am I on my own?

You get the full Opulence OS system library, adoption tracker, and documentation handed over — built to run without us. The Team Stickiness Guarantee covers the full 75 days, so if it's not holding, we're still in it with you.

What actually happens on the call?
